Pulse Garden 🌱

A calm-breathing companion. A garden that responds to your pulse, breath and expression in real time.

Before we start — camera consent

  • What it uses: your camera, only while a session is running.
  • What happens on-device: the SmartSpectra SDK computes pulse, breathing rate and facial expression locally. Raw video never leaves your machine. Pulse/breathing/expression values are never uploaded.
  • Telemetry: the SDK sends an aggregate, anonymous session summary (build, platform, counts) by default — no video, no metric values, no identifiers. Disable it below if you prefer.
  • Storage: Pulse Garden stores nothing. Close the app and the session is gone. No account, no cloud.
  • Not medical: this is a general-wellness relaxation toy. It is not a medical device and makes no diagnosis or treatment claims.

Press Start afterwards. Your camera LED will light up; the stream stays on this device.
